## Slide 1 — Title

# Job Apply Agent
### AI-Powered Job Search Automation

**Reducing repetitive career workflows through Python and AI automation.**

---

## Slide 2 — Business Context

Job seekers manage opportunities across multiple platforms, job descriptions, requirements, and application statuses.

This creates repetitive work that can take time away from skill development and interview preparation.

---

## Slide 3 — Problem Statement

Manual job-search workflows are often repetitive:

- Searching multiple sources
- Reviewing job requirements
- Comparing skills with openings
- Tracking application progress
- Managing follow-ups

---

## Slide 4 — Project Objective

Build a Python-based automation project that helps organize job-search and application-related workflows in a structured manner.

**Goal:** Reduce repetitive manual effort and create a foundation for intelligent career automation.

---

## Slide 5 — Solution Overview

Job Apply Agent provides:

- A structured Python automation workflow
- A foundation for AI-powered extensions
- Organized processing of job-related information
- An extensible project architecture
